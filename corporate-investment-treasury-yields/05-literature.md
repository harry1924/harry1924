# 五、文献地图

## 供给与期限溢价
- Greenwood, R., & Vayanos, D. (2014). Bond Supply and Excess Bond Returns. *Review of Financial Studies*. —— 美债久期供给与期限溢价的基准框架。
- Krishnamurthy, A., & Vissing-Jorgensen, A. (2012). The Aggregate Demand for Treasury Debt. *Journal of Political Economy*. —— 美债便利收益与企业债利差的关系。

## 企业发债期限与美债供给的互动
- Greenwood, R., Hanson, S., & Stein, J. (2010). A Gap-Filling Theory of Corporate Debt Maturity Choice. *Journal of Finance*. —— 美债长债供给减少时企业增发长债，H4 的直接依据。
- Badoer, D., & James, C. (2016). The Determinants of Long-Term Corporate Debt Issuances. *Journal of Finance*. —— 30 年以上企业债发行与美债供给的关系。
- Graham, J., Leary, M., & Roberts, M. (2015). A Century of Capital Structure: The Leveraging of Corporate America. *Journal of Financial Economics*. —— 百年尺度上政府债务对企业杠杆的挤出。

## 储蓄—投资与中性利率
- Bernanke, B. (2005). The Global Saving Glut and the U.S. Current Account Deficit. Sandridge Lecture.
- IMF (2006). Awash with Cash: Why Are Corporate Savings So High? *World Economic Outlook*, April, Ch. 4.
- Holston, K., Laubach, T., & Williams, J. (2017). Measuring the Natural Rate of Interest: International Trends and Determinants. *Journal of International Economics*.

## 信用条件与宏观
- Gilchrist, S., & Zakrajšek, E. (2012). Credit Spreads and Business Cycle Fluctuations. *American Economic Review*.
